Which areas does Adam Naamani serve?

Adam works with buyers, sellers, and investors across Greater Vancouver and the Lower Mainland, including Vancouver, Burnaby, New Westminster, and Port Coquitlam.

How does Adam decide what a home is worth?

Every list price and offer starts with market evidence: recent comparable sales, current competition, and how buyers and lenders view the home. That approach is backed by more than 15 years in real estate and 3,000+ Lower Mainland homes valued.

Can I get a free home valuation?

Yes. Email Adam or request a free, no-obligation comparative market analysis on Resider. Asking for a valuation does not commit you to listing.

Is Adam Naamani a licensed REALTOR®?
